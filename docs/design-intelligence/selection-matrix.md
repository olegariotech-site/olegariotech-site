## By content quality

### Strong real photography
Prefer:
- P06;
- P02;
- Apple/Airbnb logic.

### Weak or inconsistent photography
Prefer:
- stronger typography;
- P10;
- P04;
- P09;
- restrained mockups.

Do not fake premium quality with heavy gradients.

### Strong measured proof
Prefer:
- P03 prominently.

### No measured proof yet
Use:
- process;
- real project visuals;
- factual operational proof;
- no invented metrics.

## Decision rule

If two references conflict, choose the one that better serves:

1. comprehension;
2. commercial action;
3. proof;
4. accessibility;
5. client identity;
6. atmosphere.

Atmosphere loses every tie.
